FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about downloading with Grably.

Can I use Grably on my laptop or computer?

Yes. Paste a link into the box at the top of this page and pick a quality. It works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on Windows, Mac, Chromebook and Linux, with nothing to install.

Where do my downloads go?

To your browser's Downloads folder: an MP4 video that plays in QuickTime, Photos, VLC or Windows Media Player, or an MP3 if you chose audio.

Which apps does Grably work with?

TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Pinterest, Reddit, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Snapchat (Spotlight), Twitch (clips and past streams), LinkedIn, Tumblr, SoundCloud (sound only) and Bilibili. Copy a video's link from any of them and paste it into Grably. YouTube isn't supported.

Why can't I download some videos?

Private videos, videos that need a login to watch, and posts that have been removed can't be downloaded. Make sure the link points to a single public video.

When is the phone app coming?

Soon. The Grably app is on its way to the App Store and Google Play. Until then, this website works in your phone's browser too.

Is Grably free?

Yes, completely. There's no subscription, no premium plan and no sign-up. Grably is supported by the ads shown on this website, and the phone app will be free too.

Can I download any video I want?

Please only download videos you own or have permission to save, and follow the terms of the platform you're downloading from. Respect creators and their intellectual property.
