Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information Grably handles when you use the Grably website or the Grably app for Android, and how the ads on the website work.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name or email address.
  • We use the links you paste only to get your video, and we don't keep a list of what you download.
  • Downloaded videos are deleted from our server as soon as they've been sent to you.
  • Grably itself doesn't set cookies. The ads on the site come from Google AdSense, which does use cookies (see below).

Information we handle

Links you paste

When you paste a link, it's sent to our server so we can find the video and prepare your download. We don't store the links you download. If something goes wrong, the link and the error may be written to our server logs so we can fix the problem.

Your downloads

The video is saved on our server only while it's being prepared and sent to you, and it's deleted right after. If a download is interrupted, the leftover file is deleted automatically, normally within a few hours.

Your IP address

To keep Grably fair and protect it from abuse, we limit how many requests one visitor can make per minute. Your IP address is used for this and held in memory for about a minute; it isn't saved. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and reliability.

The Grably Android app

The app uses the same Grably server as the website, so everything above about links, downloads and IP addresses applies to it too. In addition:

  • No account, no ads, no analytics. The app doesn't show ads, doesn't use advertising IDs, and doesn't include analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • Your download list stays on your phone. The titles, links and thumbnails of your downloads are stored only on your device so you can find them again. Removing an item, or uninstalling the app, deletes them. They aren't backed up to the cloud.
  • Saved videos. Videos and audio you download are saved in your phone's Movies/Grably and Music/Grably folders. The app doesn't need permission to read your other photos, videos or files.
  • Clipboard. When you open the app after copying something, it checks whether you copied a video link from a supported site. If you did, the app fills it in and looks the video up, just like a link you paste. Anything else on your clipboard is ignored and never leaves your phone.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the app shows download progress and tells you when a video is saved. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Cookies and advertising

The Grably website is free because it shows ads from Google AdSense. The website itself doesn't set any cookies, but the ads do:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info (or youronlinechoices.eu in Europe).
  • To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you'll be asked for your consent before ads use cookies, and you can change your choice at any time.

Other services

To get your video, our server requests it from the site you linked to (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X or Pinterest). Those sites' own privacy policies apply to the content they host. Our fonts are hosted on our own server, so loading this website doesn't send requests to font services.

Children

Grably isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't keep personal information about you beyond what's described above, there's usually nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalized ads using the links above. If you have a question or a request, contact us.
